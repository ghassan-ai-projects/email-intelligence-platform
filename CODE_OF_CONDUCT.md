# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project is a technical community. Keep it that way:

- Focus on technical substance, not the person behind it.
- Assume good intent and ask before assuming malice.
- Give constructive, specific feedback in reviews.
- Respect that contributors work in different languages, time zones, and
  experience levels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information — including anyone's email content,
  credentials, or personal data — without explicit consent.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, reviews, or CI.
- Using project spaces for spam or self-promotion unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, close issues, or
restrict participation when this standard is violated. Enforcement decisions
are at the maintainers' discretion and are not a court of law.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers — for example through
GitHub's private reporting or a direct message to a maintainer — rather than
in public issues. Include links or screenshots when available.
